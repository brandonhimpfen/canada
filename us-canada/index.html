Methodology & Caveats

  • Goods data are on a Census basis, not seasonally adjusted.
  • Services data are annual estimates for 2024 (BEA/USTR).
  • 2025 YTD includes months available (Jan–Jun).
  • Totals may not equal sums due to rounding.
